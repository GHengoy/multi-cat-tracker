Day 4

  • Once a day is enough. Consistency matters far more than frequency, and morning or evening logging both work fine.
  • Partial data is fine. Write not observed rather than inventing entries, and never backfill, because approximate numbers are perfectly acceptable.
  • Emergencies come first. If something seems urgent, call your vet before logging, since safety comes first and the log can wait.
