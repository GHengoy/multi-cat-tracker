Day 14

  • Anchor it. Tie checks to coffee, lunch or bedtime so it takes minutes, and the whole check takes only a few minutes.
  • Log only what you saw. Leave blanks or write not observed instead of guessing, because honest gaps are better than tidy guesses.
  • Review weekly. Ten minutes once a week reveals trends daily notes miss, since a small habit beats an ambitious system you abandon.
