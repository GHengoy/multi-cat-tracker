Day 10

  • Measure the refill, not the guess. Fill the bowl to the same mark each morning and note how much it took, since evaporation is similar day to day.
  • Watch who visits. Spot checks, a pet camera or a second bowl in a quiet room show which cat keeps going back.
  • Compare each cat to their own normal. A cat who suddenly drinks or pees noticeably more is worth a call to your vet, not a wait-and-see.

Log the household refill and each cat's estimate in the tracker's Water Intake (ml) column under the right Cat Name, so download the Multi-Cat Household Health & Litter Tracker and see the change in black and white.
