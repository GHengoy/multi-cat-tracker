Day 7

  • Track the residents too. The cat already living there often shows stress first, by eating less, hiding or skipping the box.
  • Log the same four things daily. Appetite, litter box visits, hiding time and hissing or swatting give a quick read on how each cat copes.
  • Let the log set the pace. If any cat is still off their food after a day or two, step back a stage and call your vet if it continues.

Give every cat a row each day in the tracker's Appetite Level, Litter Box Visits and Behavior Notes columns, so download the Multi-Cat Household Health & Litter Tracker and see who needs more time.
