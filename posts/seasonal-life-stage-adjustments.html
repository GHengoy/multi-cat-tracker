Payoff

  • Normal shifts. Seasons, aging and life stages change what normal looks like for each cat, so compare each cat to their own baseline, not last year.
  • Note the season. A quick line in Notes gives you and your vet useful context, for example winter naps or drinking a bit less.
  • Date life events. Diet changes, moves and new cats turn your log into a timeline, and add the date for any change your vet directed.
