Step 4

  • One row per cat. Each row is one cat at one time, so patterns stay separate, spelling each cat's name the same way every time.
  • Describe, don't interpret. Write what you saw and leave the meaning to your vet, using plain words like soft, reduced or lethargic.
  • Estimates are fine. Food and water columns are for spotting patterns, not lab precision, so nobody needs a kitchen laboratory at home.
