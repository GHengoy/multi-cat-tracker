Day 14

  • Reduce competition. Per-cat feeders, extra water and enough roomy boxes give each cat space, so shared spots stop being a source of stress.
  • Add vertical space. Cat trees and window perches let cats claim territory peacefully, and solo space matters as much as shared space.
  • Track what changes. A product's real value is what you notice afterwards, then watch what changes after each addition.
