Day 10

  • Guilty looks aren't evidence. The cat sitting nearby is often just curious, so write down who was actually in the room.
  • Note time, place and contents. Undigested food soon after a meal, a hairball or a favorite spot quickly points to one cat.
  • Count it per cat. Once you know who, repeated vomiting, or vomiting with low appetite or energy, is worth a call to your vet.
