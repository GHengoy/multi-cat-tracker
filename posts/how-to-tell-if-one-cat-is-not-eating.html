Day 4

  • Shared bowls hide it. An empty bowl only shows the group ate, not that every cat did, because a bigger appetite can cover for a smaller one.
  • Watch one meal. Give each cat a bowl and pick up leftovers after 20 to 30 minutes, and space the bowls apart so nobody gets crowded out.
  • Write it per cat. A cat who eats little for about a day deserves a call to your vet, since a quick note each meal shows the pattern.
