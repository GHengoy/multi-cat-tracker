## Candidates considered

**Guinea Pig Tracking** - Smaller niche with growing community (r/guineapigs exists) and some Etsy products (weight trackers, weekly care logs). Evidence: Multiple PDF trackers available on Etsy. Lower competition than dogs/cats but significantly smaller addressable market. Rejected due to smaller audience size compared to multi-cat households.

**Rabbit Care Tracking** - More established ecosystem with existing players like Everbreed, My House Rabbit (Domain Authority 73), and BunnySync platform. Evidence: 20+ established rabbit blogs listed, specialized rabbit management platforms already serving breeders and owners. Too saturated with existing solutions.

**Senior Dog Health Tracking** - Growing demographic with clear tracking needs (weight, appetite, bathroom habits, medication). Evidence: DogLog app exists, veterinary checklists available, specific pain point documented. Good signal but DogLog already addresses the core use case. Market opportunity exists but less differentiated than multi-cat households.

**Hedgehog Care Tracking** - Growing market: feed market alone valued at $150M in 2025, projected to reach $250M by 2034 with 8.2% CAGR. Evidence: Market reports show rising urban pet ownership, demand for specialized care products. Niche is expanding and underserved with digital products. However, smaller addressable market than multi-cat households.

**Multi-Cat Household Health & Litter Tracking** - **SELECTED.** Large addressable market (millions of multi-cat households worldwide). Evidence: Documented specific pain point - "one of the biggest challenges in multi-cat households is identifying which cat has abnormal toileting behavior" (International Cat Care, Litter Robot). Pet camera demand surging in multi-cat homes per 2025 pet tech reports. PurrTrack app exists but only recently created (emerging competition, not saturated). Clear tracking needs: litter box frequency per cat, stool quality, appetite, water intake, individual medication. No major existing digital tracker dominates this specific angle.

## Why this niche

Multi-cat households represent a large, underserved market with a **specific, painful tracking problem**: owners cannot identify which cat has health or litter issues when living with multiple cats. Web search revealed this is documented as "the multi-cat mental load" with real solutions emerging (PurrTrack, litter box cameras with AI cat recognition). The niche has strong demand signals (pet camera surge in multi-cat homes, expert recommendations for close monitoring per cat), but is not yet saturated with digital trackers. Content can stay strictly in routine/observational tracking territory—feeding schedules, litter frequency, stool quality observations, medication timing, water intake, activity level—without requiring any medical diagnosis or veterinary advice claims. The product directly solves the problem: "which cat had litter issues?" and "is individual cat X eating/hydrating normally?" without crossing into medical territory.
